# Rule: Corporate B2B Design System & Information Architecture

## UI/UX Mandates
- **Typography:** Use highly legible, modern sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter or Roboto).
- **Spacing:** Apply ample whitespace (padding/margins) to avoid clutter, utilizing Tailwind's spacing scale effectively.

## Services Categorization (CRITICAL)
When generating the capabilities section, NEVER render a flat list. You must group the client's 14 services into the following categorized CSS Grid layout using clean, white cards with subtle drop shadows:

**1. Strategy & Advisory**
- Strategy & Management Advisory
- ESG & Sustainability Advisory
- Financial & Investment Services
- Investment & New Business Venture Advisory & Partnerships

**2. Technology & Innovation**
- IT & Digital Transformation Services
- Engineering Solutions & Technology Development 
- AI, Automation & Data Enablement
- Cybersecurity & Risk Management
- Emerging & Niche Technology Services (Blockchain, IoT, Fractional CXO, etc.)

**3. Operations & Talent**
- IT Staffing & Workforce Solutions
- Operations & Process Optimization
- Marketing & Customer Experience Strategy

**4. Specialized Industries**
- Industry-Specific Advisory Services
- Medical Services & Healthcare Advisory & Expansion
