# Evezas Static Landing Page

## Brand Identity
- **Company Name:** Evezas
- **Logo Asset:** `image_b2ff23.png`
- **Color Palette:** Corporate Navy Blue (primary), Silver/Slate Grey (secondary), Crisp White (backgrounds).
- **Vibe:** Highly professional, trustworthy, enterprise-grade advisory firm.

## Reference Architecture
Mimic the clean, structural spacing and modern B2B feel of:
- https://insightglobal.com/industries/telecom/
- https://www.baringa.com/en/capabilities/technology/?

## Page Structure
1. **Navigation:** Logo on the left, standard links (Home, Capabilities, Industries, Contact) on the right.
2. **Hero Section:** Dark blue overlay, striking transformation-focused headline, and a primary CTA.
3. **Capabilities Grid:** The core services layout (see design rules).
4. **Footer:** Contact info, quick links, and branding.
